Dear Prachi,

Twenty years ago you showed up and made our family louder, funnier, and a whole lot brighter. I still can't believe I get to call you my sister — my built-in best friend, my partner in every bad decision and every good one.

Watching you grow into who you are today has been one of my favorite things to witness. You're kind without trying to be, funny without meaning to be, and stronger than you give yourself credit for. Whatever this new year brings, I hope it's full of the things that make you laugh the hardest and the people who love you the most.

Here's to twenty — and to every birthday after this one, because I plan on annoying you at every single one.

Love, your sibling 💛
